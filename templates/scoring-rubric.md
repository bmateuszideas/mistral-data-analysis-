Rules: weights must be explicit and sum to 100; every score needs a justification tied to evidence; use a worded scale where possible (Very Low / Low / Neutral / Good / Very Good); mark unknowns as "Not found".

Example weights (candidate screening): Technical skills 40, Experience 30, Education 15, Additional qualifications 15.
